📋 Overview

This page explains how to delete your SmartSpend account and all associated data. We respect your right to privacy and make complete data deletion straightforward.

⚠️ Attention: Irreversible Action

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible.

All your data will be removed from our servers, including:

  • User profile
  • Transaction history
  • Budget categories
  • Financial goals
  • Settings & Preferences
  • Premium status

🎯 Method 1: From the App

The simplest and fastest way:

1
Open SmartSpend App

Launch the app on your Android or iOS device.

2
Access Profile

Tap profile icon or open side menu and select "My Profile".

3
Account Actions

Scroll to "Account Actions" section at bottom of page.

4
Delete Account

Tap the red "Delete Account" button.

5
Confirmation

Read warning. Enter password if prompted to confirm.

6
Final Validation

Tap "Delete" to confirm permanently.

📧 Method 2: By Email

If you cannot access the app:

1
Email Support

Contact us at heyhappyproject@gmail.com

2
Required Info

Subject: "Account Deletion Request". Specify your associated email address.

3
Processing Time

Processed within 30 days max. You will receive confirmation.

🗑️ Permanently Deleted Data

Personal Data

  • Profile (Name, email, photo)
  • Login credentials
  • Preferences and notifications

Financial Data

  • Full transaction history
  • Budgets and categories
  • Goals and statistics

📝 Temporary Retention

ℹ️ Legal Obligations
  • Billing: 10 years for accounting obligations (EU).
  • Security Logs: 1 year for fraud prevention.
  • Anonymized Data: Aggregated non-identifiable statistics.

⏱️ Deletion Timelines

  • ⚡ Instant: Account deactivation.
  • ⏳ 48 hours: Active database removal.
  • 📅 30 days: Full backups deletion.

🔄 Partial Deletion

You can delete specific items without closing your account:

  • ✓ Individual transactions via "Transactions" tab.
  • ✓ Budget categories via "Budget" tab.
  • ✓ Goals via "Goals" tab.

⚖️ Your Rights (GDPR)

In accordance with applicable laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right to erasure
  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to portability
  • Right to object
